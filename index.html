Bibliography Vizualized

Teaching tools for how books were made and how bibliographers describe them.

Hand-Press Printing House

Set mirrored type from a historical case, impose folio, quarto or octavo formes on the stone, print both sides on a hand press, then fold the sheet into a book.

Open the printing house →

Collational Formula Workbench

Gather and sign gatherings, mark cancels and insertions, and bind them to see the book's collational formula and signing statement, following Bowers.

Open the workbench →

Bookbinding Structures Workbench

How books were bound in the West, from the Coptic codex to the publisher's cloth case. Pick a period to see its structure and materials, watch quires being sewn, then follow a binding step by step from folded sheets to finished book.

Open the bindery →
Both tools run entirely in the browser. No data is collected or stored.
