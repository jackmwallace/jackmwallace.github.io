Printed for the Use of Students

Bibliography studies books as material objects: how they were set in type, printed, folded, gathered, sewn and bound, and how a bibliographer records all of this in a precise description. These instruments let you work through each stage for yourself, from the compositor's case to the finished description of a copy.

The First Instrument

The Printing House

Compose a line of type, impose it in folio, quarto or octavo, pull sheets on a hand press and fold them into a book.

The Collational Formula

Bookbinding Structures

Follow Western binding from the Coptic codex to the publisher's cloth case: sewing, boards, covering and decoration.
